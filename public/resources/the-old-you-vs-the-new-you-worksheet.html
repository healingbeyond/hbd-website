The Old You vs. The New You

After a life-changing injury, illness, diagnosis or transition, your sense of identity may change. This worksheet is a place to notice what has shifted, what remains, and what you may want to carry forward. There are no right answers.

You do not have to become who you were before. You can acknowledge what you miss while making room for who you are now.

1 · Looking back

Which roles, routines, strengths or parts of your identity felt important before things changed?

What do you miss, and what feelings come up when you think about it?

2 · Where I am now

What has changed in the way you see yourself or move through everyday life?

Which parts of you are still here, even if they show up differently?

3 · Making room for both

What from your past do you want to keep, honour or adapt?

What expectations—your own or someone else's—might you be ready to set aside?

4 · Moving forward on my terms

What matters to you today? What would a meaningful next step look like for you?

What support, space or understanding would help?

Final reflection: What would you say to someone who believes you have to return to the old you to be recovering?
